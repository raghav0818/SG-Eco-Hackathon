# How to show the waste you actually diverted

## What the programme counts

The Biggest Impact total is the measured weight of **food waste, plastic waste, or both** kept from landfill during the programme. “Diverted” includes **reduced, repurposed, or recycled**, and this activity does **not** have to come from the prototype. The target is at least **20 kg per team**. ([Kickoff Slides, pp. 5–6](../hackathon%20documents/20260826%20SG%20Eco%20Loop%20Kickoff%20Slides.pdf); [Day 1 Slides, p. 7](../hackathon%20documents/20260905%20SG%20Eco%20Loop%20Hackathon%20Day%201%20Slides.pdf))

For this project, use two honest evidence streams:

1. **Smart-tray trial — food waste reduced:** compare end-of-day unsold food before and after the stall acts on the tray observations.
2. **Separate real diversion — food composting or plastic recycling:** the programme expressly allows diversion unrelated to the prototype. This is the simplest way to build a clearly verifiable kilogram total.

## Evidence set for every batch

Give each batch an ID such as `2026-09-24-F01`, then keep these together:

1. **Collection photo:** the actual food or plastic, with the batch label, date, and location visible.
2. **Weighing photo:** the whole batch on a scale with the number readable. Record kilograms and subtract the empty container or bag weight. The organisers specifically ask participants to weigh the waste before recycling and photograph the scale reading. ([Kickoff transcript, 02:36:11–02:36:57 and 02:38:43–02:38:54](../hackathon%20documents/20260826%20SG%20Eco%20Loop%20Kickoff.transcript.vtt))
3. **Destination photo or confirmation:** show the real next step: delivery to a composter or recipient, deposit with a plastic off-taker, a reverse-vending receipt, or the recycling drop point. An off-taker that actually uses the material is preferred; a blue recycling bin is accepted as a fallback. ([Kickoff transcript, 02:42:52–02:43:34](../hackathon%20documents/20260826%20SG%20Eco%20Loop%20Kickoff.transcript.vtt))
4. **Short write-up:** state what the waste was, where it came from, how it was collected, its net weight, what you did with it, and where it ended up. This is exactly the level of detail requested by the form introduction. ([Waste Diary form, first screenshot](./image.png))

Keep the original photo files. Do not use generated, staged, or materially edited images as evidence. Rankings are based on verified submissions and the programme stresses integrity; its AI guidance also says AI must not replace real interactions and that participants must be able to explain their submissions. ([Waste Diary form, first screenshot](./image.png); [Framework Reference, p. 1](../hackathon%20documents/SG%20Eco%20Loop%20Hackathon%20Framework%20Reference.pdf); [Kickoff transcript, 00:16:44–00:18:13](../hackathon%20documents/20260826%20SG%20Eco%20Loop%20Kickoff.transcript.vtt))

## How to measure the smart-tray reduction

Use comparable stall days and the same weighing method throughout:

| Stage | What to record |
|---|---|
| Baseline | For at least 3 comparable days before changing production, weigh all end-of-day unsold food. Record operating hours and portions or sales. |
| Intervention | Use the smart-tray observations to make one recorded change, such as preparing a smaller final batch of a slow-selling dish. For at least 3 comparable days, weigh the remaining unsold food the same way. |
| Claim | `baseline average waste per comparable day − trial average waste per comparable day`, multiplied only by the number of eligible trial days. Claim zero for any day where the trial result is worse. |

For each trial day, photograph the tray near closing, the leftovers on the scale, and the written preparation change. Attach the stall’s acknowledgement if possible. In the explanation, state that this is an **estimated reduction based on a before-and-after comparison**, identify the dates, and show the calculation. Do not count the same food again if the remaining leftovers are later composted or repurposed.

This method is a defensible way to quantify “reduced” waste; the programme documents do not prescribe a formal baseline formula. The Scale-Up Plan separately asks for the impact diverted using the prototype. ([Framework Reference, p. 1](../hackathon%20documents/SG%20Eco%20Loop%20Hackathon%20Framework%20Reference.pdf))

## Easiest additional diversion routes

- **Food:** weigh unavoidable preparation scraps or unsold food, then photograph their handover to a composting partner, home-composting step, or real recipient who can use the food. The organisers give composting and redirecting food to another user as accepted examples. ([Kickoff transcript, 02:36:33–02:36:57 and 02:37:42–02:38:10](../hackathon%20documents/20260826%20SG%20Eco%20Loop%20Kickoff.transcript.vtt))
- **Plastic:** collect only accepted clean plastic, rinse and dry it, weigh it before drop-off, and photograph the recycling route. The organisers warn that dirty containers may be rejected and incinerated. ([Kickoff transcript, 02:36:57–02:37:42](../hackathon%20documents/20260826%20SG%20Eco%20Loop%20Kickoff.transcript.vtt))

Do not create or buy extra waste to increase the total. ([Kickoff transcript, 00:16:44–00:18:13](../hackathon%20documents/20260826%20SG%20Eco%20Loop%20Kickoff.transcript.vtt))

## What to enter in the Waste Diary

Submit **every week**. The visible required fields are:

- name and email of submitter;
- team name (`NA` if solo) and school/institution;
- reporting-period start and end dates;
- Plastic Waste, Food Waste, or both;
- the category-specific evidence requested after selection; and
- optional organiser remarks.

The form introduction requires photo evidence and a short explanation, while the programme slides require every kilogram to be logged with a weigh-in and photo. ([Waste Diary form screenshots](./image.png) and [second screenshot](<./image copy.png>); [Kickoff Slides, p. 6](../hackathon%20documents/20260826%20SG%20Eco%20Loop%20Kickoff%20Slides.pdf))

The two screenshots were taken before a waste category was selected, so they do not show the conditional kilogram, upload, or write-up fields. Select the relevant category and complete every field that appears. If no such fields appear, place the net weight, calculation, batch IDs, route, and evidence summary in the remarks box and contact `sgecoloop@sdta.org.sg` before relying on that submission.
