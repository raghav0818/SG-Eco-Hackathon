## 1. Interview request — send to 8–10 people (WhatsApp/Telegram)

Target n=5–8 replies. Answers Q2–Q5 in `00-master-plan.md` §10, plus the new sinks from `01-problem-map.md`.

**The three that matter most: 2, 7 and 8.** Q2 — if Type 1 plates already come in size variation, candidate A1 collapses. Q7 sizes W7 (scanned, not taken), which nothing currently measures. Q8 is the only way to separate W5 (didn’t want the food) from W6 (wanted less of it) — they land in the same bin and weight alone cannot tell them apart.

Keep it casual. A formal survey kills the reply rate.

> Hey — doing a food waste project for a hackathon and I need cookhouse input from people who've actually eaten there. Six quick questions, voice note or one-liners totally fine, no rush but before Sat would be amazing:
>
> 1. Which camp/cookhouse, and is the food already plated up under the heat lamps when you get there, or do they serve it onto your tray? Does it change by meal?
> 2. If it's pre-plated — are the plates all the same size, or is there any choice? Are there ever plates left sitting there at the end, and what happens to them?
> 3. Have you ever wanted less than what was on the plate? What did you actually do — force it down, bin it, give it to someone?
> 4. Can you go back for seconds? Does anyone actually do it? What stops people?
> 5. Roughly what fraction of plates go back with food still on them? Just your impression, not a count.
> 6. Does anyone — the cooks, the servers, the CSM — ever find out how much food came back?
> 7. Do you ever scan your 11B and then not actually take the food? How often, and why?
> 7b. Are people ever *told* to scan even when they’re not eating, so the numbers tally?
> 7c. Who does the food indent for your unit, and is it a weekly number? What happens if it doesn’t tally — who gets the report?
> 8. When you don’t finish, is it usually the food itself (not nice, same thing again) or just your appetite that day?
>
> No names, nothing identifying, not photographing anything. Just trying to understand how the portion gets decided. Thanks!

**Log every person who replies in the People Log** — the Scale-Up Plan explicitly asks who you tested with. It's scored, not admin.
